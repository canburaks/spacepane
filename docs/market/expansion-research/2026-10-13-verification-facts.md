# Verification of claims behind the expansion ranking

Checked 2026-10-13 (Europe/Istanbul)

## Method and limits

- I tried to refute each claim by opening a primary source (law text, tax authority, vendor repository LICENSE file, Apple documentation, the vendor's own advisory). CONFIRMED means I opened a page that says it. A search-result summary alone is never CONFIRMED.
- Tools: WebSearch and WebFetch only. WebFetch passes each page through a small summarising model, so every "what the source says" cell is that summary plus at most one short phrase. Counts (Debian CVEs, Modrinth downloads) were read by that model from the page; recount before printing them.
- Pages that blocked or failed: finance.belgium.be (CAPTCHA, not bypassed), bundesfinanzministerium.de (HTTP 503 three times), economie.gouv.fr (403), ksef.podatki.gov.pl and isap.sejm.gov.pl (DNS failure), autodesk.com blog (403), api.github.com (403, rate limit), koi.ai (redirects to a Palo Alto Networks page), developer.garmin.com/fit/download (404 on both URL forms), nvd.nist.gov (page did not render). Two GİB PDFs and the Hancom HWP PDF came back as compressed binary; WebFetch saved them under the tool-results folder, which is outside the report folder, so I did not read them.
- I used about 140 tool calls, over the 70 to 100 budget. Nothing in groups A to H was skipped, but several items ended as COULD NOT VERIFY (listed at the end).
- No file other than this one was written. No commits.

Verdict counts (69 items): CONFIRMED 52, PARTLY 8, REFUTED 3, COULD NOT VERIFY 6.
