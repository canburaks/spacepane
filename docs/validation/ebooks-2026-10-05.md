## Findings made by the tests

- **Escaping defeated privacy.** The first converter escaped every Markdown underscore. The pipeline privacy test
  showed `api_key = synthetic-secret-value` surviving as `api\_key = …`, because the shared filter recognizes the key by
  its spelling. Fixed by leaving intraword underscores literal; pinned by converter and pipeline tests. Bit-flipped
  fixtures can rewrite a secret's key (`token` becomes `tok?n`), so, as in the adversarial corpus, only truncations are
  held to the concealment guarantee.
- **Renderer limits bind generated text.** A 240,000-unit book with a blank line after every short paragraph exceeded the
  Rendered view's 20,000-line limit; the line ceiling and the unit ceiling are both enforced by the writer and tested.
- **Tiny budgets are impractical for ZIP formats.** The ZIP reader charges its tail window and central directory to the
  cumulative read budget, so tests derive budgets from measured reads instead of fixed small numbers.
- **Notebook figures are limited by the shared text path** (see the decision record); a figure over about 24 KiB of
  PNG still fails the notebook as `lineTooLong`, unchanged from before.

## Not verified

- Finder routing, provider priority and actual Quick Look hosting for `.cbz`, `.epub`, `.fb2`, `.mht` and `.mhtml`;
  none of the imported or system type declarations has been observed to win. Companion parsing and `qlmanage` are not
  evidence for the Finder surfaces and `qlmanage` was not run.
- Thumbnails: this work only sets the policy `content` and exposes `TextPresentation.images`; nothing was drawn or
  invoked. The thumbnail extension does not use these presentations in this branch.
- VoiceOver output, focus and scrolling behavior, keyboard handling inside the remote Quick Look host, Reduce Motion
  and high-contrast appearance of the Pages view, and layout in right-to-left languages.
- Native-speaker review of the 25 new strings (8 format names/descriptions, 17 interface strings). Translations are
  drafts.
- Release-configuration performance: time and memory were not measured. The decode cap (4,096 px, 8 megapixels), the
  192 MiB resident-bitmap budget and the two-decode concurrency are bounds by construction, not measured peaks. Cold and
  warm requests and the sandboxed extension's real memory ceiling were not recorded.
- WebP, HEIC and AVIF pages and covers: the validator's signatures were tested with structurally invalid samples only,
  because ImageIO on the test machine cannot encode them here; real files in those formats were not decoded.
- Real-world books, comics and saved pages. Fixtures are synthetic by rule, so DRM schemes other than the descriptors
  named in the decision (Adobe `rights.xml`/`encryption.xml`, Apple `sinf.xml`) and generator quirks are untested.
- Behavior on the integrated tree: shared files (`SafePreviewModes`, `PresentationNotices`, the catalog, strings and the
  localization test set) must be merged with the other families, then these commands re-run there.
